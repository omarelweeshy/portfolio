01 The idea

We don't build puzzles on walls. We rebuild moments from history, down to the dust on the table, then we lock you inside them.

  • i.

    Hand-built

    Real stone, brass and timber. No tablets on the wall, no printed props.

  • ii.

    A live game master

    Someone watches every move and steps in only when you need a nudge.

  • iii.

    Story first

    Every room has a beginning, a twist and exactly one way out.

06 Questions

Are we actually locked in?

No. The exit door can always be opened from inside and there is an emergency button in every room. The game master can stop the game at any moment.

Is it scary?

It is atmospheric, not horror. Low light, real fire effects (safe ones) and sound, but nothing jumps out at you.

How old do players need to be?

Twelve and up. Players under sixteen need an adult on the team.

Do you host company team building?

Yes. Run two rooms side by side for up to twelve people, with a debrief afterwards on how each team communicated.

Where are you?

Fifth Settlement, New Cairo, five minutes from the 90th Street. Parking on site. (This is a concept venue.)
