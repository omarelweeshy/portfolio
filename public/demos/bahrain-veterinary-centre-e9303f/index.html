    Note for the owner: a lighter, faster homepage

    The current homepage weighs about 7.9 MB, mostly from slider images that are still the theme's placeholder files (dummy.png). On a phone over mobile data that means several seconds of waiting before anything useful appears. The page also has no meta description, so search engines invent their own snippet.

    Quick fixes (about a day of work)

    • Remove the dummy.png slider images, or replace them with two or three real clinic photos.
    • Resize photos to the size they are shown at and compress them to WebP (typically under 150 KB each).
    • Lazy-load images below the fold so the first screen appears straight away.
    • Add a meta description naming the clinic, Maqabah and the main services.
    • Put this appointment request form back online so routine bookings move off the phone.

    Target: a homepage under 1 MB that shows useful content in about a second on 4G.
