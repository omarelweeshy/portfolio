Review memo · online appointment form

Your appointment form asks for more than it needs

The online appointment form on montherdental.com asks patients for their CPR (ID) number and date of birth before they can request a visit, and there is no privacy notice explaining why, who sees it, or how long it is kept. Bookings are not confirmed back to the patient, so they cannot check what happens next.

What we observed

  • Sensitive CPR number collected for a simple appointment request.
  • Sensitive Date of birth collected up front, before any visit.
  • Missing No privacy notice next to the form.
  • Missing No consent text or tick box.
  • Missing No confirmation by email or SMS, and no booking reference to track.
  • Note The Arabic pages look dated (newest article seen is from 2015).

What we recommend

  1. Drop the CPR number from the online form, or ask for it only at the first visit at reception. If it must be kept, ask for the last 4 digits only.
  2. Drop the date of birth online; an age range or nothing at all is enough to book.
  3. Add a short privacy notice: what is collected, why, who sees it and how long it is kept.
  4. Add consent text with a tick box the patient must check before sending.
  5. Send a confirmation email or SMS (or WhatsApp) with a booking reference the patient can track.
  6. Next step: refresh the Arabic site so both languages match.
