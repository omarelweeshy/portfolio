    Quick website clean-up fixes

    1. One business name. The site mixes "United Movers & Packers" and "Bahrain Experts Movers". Pick one main name for the logo, page titles and Google listing, and mention the other once as "also known as".
    2. A real quote form. Replace the phone/WhatsApp-only "Request your quote" call to action with a short form like this one, so every lead is saved, tracked and followed up.
    3. Mobile layout. Check every page at phone width: no sideways scrolling, tap targets at least 44px, and the quote button visible without hunting.
    4. Tidy service pages. One clear page per service (house moves, office moves, packing, furniture assembly) with real photos, areas covered and what is included.
    5. Consistent contact details. Show the same phone, WhatsApp and address on every page and in the footer, matching the Google Business profile.
    6. Page titles and descriptions. Give each page a unique title such as "Movers and packers in Bahrain | United Movers & Packers" to help search results.
