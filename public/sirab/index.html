00 / The idea

The desert keeps no record of who crosses it. No signal, no roads, no queue. Only wind, stone, and a horizon that refuses to end. We take small groups out there, read the sand for them, and bring them back changed.

Est. as a concept, 2026 Cairo · Siwa · Gilf Kebir
