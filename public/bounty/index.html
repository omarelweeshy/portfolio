01 / Paint

Paint that moves like paint.

Gloss, metallic, pearl, matte, chrome. Each finish is a physically based clear coat over a base layer, so highlights roll across the body the way they do under a showroom rig. Change colour and it wipes on, front to back.

Arctic silver · Metallic

02 / Wheels

Six wheels. Zero downloads.

Every rim is generated in code: spoke count, dish depth, concave face, polished lip. Swap them and the calipers stay put while the wheel turns, like the real thing.

Five-spoke

03 / Stance & livery

Lower. Wider. Louder.

Ride height, negative camber, window tint, plate text and vinyls projected onto the body as you pick them. Then it all goes onto a wanted poster you can share.

Ride height -20 mm · camber 2.0°

04 / Run
